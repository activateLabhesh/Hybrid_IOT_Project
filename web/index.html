      Sensor calibration
      Live soil raw: -- · rain raw: --

      Resistive probe: calibrate in your own field soil, not air or water. Tap "dry" when the soil is at its driest, and "wet" about 30 minutes after a thorough watering. Higher raw = drier. If you add fertilizer, re-check the "wet" value, because salts change the reading.

      Pump & protection

      There is no flow sensor, so litres are estimated as pump rate × running time. To measure the rate, run the pump into a bucket for 1 minute, or read the "Tank dropped … pump ≈ … L/min" line in the Log after a run. The tank sensor protects the pump: if the tank level doesn't fall while pumping, the pump is dry or blocked. Set dry-run check to 0 if the tank is refilled while you irrigate.
